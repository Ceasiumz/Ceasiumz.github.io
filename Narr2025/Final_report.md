
# I. Review on Self
## I.1. Intro
-  The Vet is an event driven 2D RPG. You have the chance to play as Doctor Blank, a mob doctor without legit identities but do operations for the underground world.  
- You need explore the world and yourself to find your lost medical skills and memory.
## I.2. Review
- The Vet  is a  simulation game about weighing and saving lives. 
- The interest points set on 3 aspects: identity, event, behaviour
	- identity: As Doc. Blank, you can start all kinds of dialogue with the weirdest patient to find out their problems—both physically and psychologically. Meanwhile, you can choose to be faithful to your patients, or you can always show your fangs, and double cross your patients.
	- event: The unit of game progression is an operation instead of a mission, but each operation is driven by emergent events. You never know what kind of patient you will get, or what kind of complications will arise during surgery. Adapt your strategy on the fly to deal with as many lives as possible.
	- behaviour: You have access to a wide range of medical tools and equipment to perform surgeries. Each tool has its own unique function and can be used in different ways to achieve the desired outcome. Master the use of these tools to become a skilled surgeon in the chaotic world of The Vet.

### II.1 Nine Loong disks
1. Attractions(Cultural settings)
	- Game sets its cultural background at 9th decade of HongKong, highlights external prosperity while internal decay of HongKong that time. Game scenes show rich material life(luxury goods) and sharp mental problems(drags, gangsters)
	- Game through the cultural collision between the east and west to deepen its inside expression. The two protagonists are Chinese traditional Daoist(FengShuiShi), and Western detective(policeman). Their interactivities bursts more complex attraction elements.
2. Expansibility(Stories and Endings)
	- Game have many corresponding clues, which may be combined with each other through different orders and triggers. These countless combinations lead to countless endings and possibilities for story development for developers to supplement.
3. prospects(interactive novel)
	- Game can be seen as one form of interactive novel illustrated in hand drawn art style. Due to very limited interactivity(innovation) aside from pure narrative design, It should be hard for this game to attract complicated player community. But still can be success if with outstanding story lines and characters.
